Dear Editors,

Please find enclosed the manuscript entitled
\textbf{“A Structural Spline-Penalized Tail Bound for L-Functions”}
by \textbf{Akbar Akbari Esfahani}.

The paper introduces the \emph{Spline-Penalized Tail Bound (SPTB)}, a
finite-window functional that reads out the off-critical-zero oscillation of
the explicit-formula remainder $G_\sigma(t)=e^{-\sigma t}(\psi(e^t)-e^t)$.
The main results establish an unconditional polynomial bound in the on-line
(variance) regime and exponential growth in the off-line (detection) regime;
the detection direction is a finite-window read-out of the classical
Tur\'an--Pintz $\Omega$-oscillation theory.  Numerical experiments using
Odlyzko's publicly available zero data confirm the predicted polynomial
vs.\ exponential transition, with measured exponential slopes matching the
theoretical value $2(\beta-\sigma)$ to within about $10^{-3}$ relative error.
A geometric reformulation interprets the functional as curvature energy on a
variable-curvature manifold, motivating the conjectural converse.

The work contributes:
  \begin{itemize}
\item A computable analytic functional with explicit blockwise constants.
\item A finite-window detector statistic for off-critical zeros, verified numerically.
\item A curvature-geometric heuristic motivating the (open) Horocycle Conjecture.
\end{itemize}

The framework packages the classical oscillation phenomenon as a
\emph{measurable, finite-window diagnostic}, fully reproducible from publicly
available data.

I believe this manuscript will interest readers of
\emph{Advances in Mathematics} for its combination of analytic rigor,
computability, and geometric originality.

Thank you for your consideration.
