\begin{abstract}
The Riemann--von Mangoldt explicit formula expands the $\sigma$-tilted remainder
$G_\sigma(t)=e^{-\sigma t}(\psi(e^t)-e^t)$ as a sum of terms
$|\rho|^{-1}e^{(\beta-\sigma)t}\cos(\gamma t+\varphi_\rho)$ over the zeros $\rho$;
an off-critical zero ($\beta>\sigma$) thus forces exponential $\Omega$-oscillation
by classical Tur\'an--Pintz theory. We study the Spline--Penalized Tail Bound (SPTB),
a finite-window positive functional $F_\lambda$, on a tractable square-summable
surrogate $H_\sigma$ of $G_\sigma$. We prove that $F_\lambda$ obeys an
unconditional polynomial bound $O(T\log T\log\log T)$ when all zeros satisfy
$\beta\le\sigma$, and grows like $e^{2(\beta-\sigma)T}$ otherwise---the latter a
read-out of $\Omega$-oscillation. Our contribution is a finite-window numerical
diagnostic on top of known oscillation theory (slope agreement within $0.03\%$ at
$T=5\times10^4$), with the converse (the ``Horocycle Conjecture'') left open.
\end{abstract}
